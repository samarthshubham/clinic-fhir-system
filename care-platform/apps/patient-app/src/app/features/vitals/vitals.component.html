Systolic (top) Diastolic (bottom) Healthy range
What does this mean?
Your blood pressure has two numbers. The top number (systolic) is the pressure when your heart beats; the bottom (diastolic) is between beats. Under 130/80 is great. Above 140/90 is high — your doctor may suggest changes if you stay there.
